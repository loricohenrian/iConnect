Group Plan

How to Buy a Group Plan

  1. Choose Plan & Devices: Select your desired plan and choose how many people will share it (minimum 2).
  2. Insert Total Coins: Tap Insert Coins and drop the required total amount into the vendo machine.
  3. Connect / Extend First: Tap Connect Now or Extend Now to activate your group session.
  4. Get Your Group Code: Once connected, your unique 5-character group code (e.g. A1B2C) will appear right on your session screen.
  5. Share With Friends: Have each friend connect to the Wi-Fi, tap "Have a group code? Enter it here", enter your code, and tap Join! Each person gets their own full session.

How to Use iConnect

Follow these steps to get connected

1

Tap "Insert Coins"

On the Home page, tap the Insert Coins 🪙 button. You can also tap View WiFi Rates & Speeds to see all coin denominations, durations, and bonus upgrade rates.

2

Insert Coins

Drop your coins (₱1, ₱5, ₱10, ₱20) into the machine during the 2-minute safety countdown. The screen shows your total inserted amount and automatically calculates your internet time using smart combo rates.

3

Tap "Connect Now"

Once you have inserted your coins, tap the Connect Now button to immediately start your internet session (or wait for the countdown to automatically connect you).

4

Pause or Extend Anytime

Need to attend class or sleep? Tap Pause Session to freeze your timer (saved for 24 to 72 hours). Want more time? Tap Insert Coins 🪙 on your session screen to extend and earn more pause chances!

How to Use Group Plan

1

Create the Group

Tap Buy Group Plan, choose your plan, and input how many people will connect. Tap Insert Coins 🪙.

2

Insert Coins & Connect

Insert the total required coins. Once complete, tap Connect Now. Your timer will start and you will receive a 5-digit Group Code.

3

Share the Code

Tell the 5-digit Group Code to your friends or family members who are joining.

4

Join the Group

Your group members must open the portal, tap Have a group code? Enter it here, type the code, and tap Join Group to get internet access.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Tips

Quick answers to common questions about your WiFi session

Why did my timer disappear, and how do I prevent it?

English: Newer phones (iOS 14+ and Android 10+) enable "Randomized MAC" by default. Because sessions are linked to your MAC address, if your phone rotates its MAC or you switch MAC settings, the system sees a new device with 0 minutes.

💡 Best Practice: Set your Wi-Fi to "Device MAC / Phone MAC" BEFORE inserting coins so your phone keeps the same permanent identity.

🔄 Lost your time? If you recently toggled your Wi-Fi privacy settings, switch back to the setting you had when you inserted coins (e.g. Randomized MAC), turn Wi-Fi off and on, and your timer will return! If your phone rotated its MAC on its own, send a message using the Report form below and the operator will restore your minutes.

Tagalog: Naka-on ang "Randomized MAC" sa mga bagong phone. Tip: Piliin ang "Device MAC" BAGO maghulog ng barya para permanente ang iyong identity. Kung nawala ang oras mo matapos galawin ang Wi-Fi settings, ibalik ito sa dati mong setting nung naghulog ka (at i-off/on ang Wi-Fi) para bumalik ang oras, o magpadala ng report sa ibaba para maibalik ng operator!

Does my time keep running if I turn off Wi-Fi or leave?

English: Yes, your session timer counts down continuously in real-time unless you pause it. If you need to attend class, sleep, or step away, always tap "Pause Session" on your portal screen before disconnecting to freeze your minutes!

Tagalog: Opo, tuloy-tuloy ang bawas ng oras maliban kung i-pause mo ito. Pindutin ang "Pause" sa session screen bago umalis para hindi masayang ang iyong internet.

How does Pause work, and do I get more pauses when I extend?

English: Pausing freezes your remaining minutes so you can use them later. Paused time is safely kept for up to 24 to 72 hours (depending on your plan). YES! Whenever you extend your session with a new coin or plan, you automatically earn new pause chances!

Tagalog: Pwedeng i-pause ang oras hanggang 24-72 oras. Oo! Kapag naghulog ka ulit ng barya para mag-extend, madadagdagan muli ang iyong pause chances!

Why did the "Insert Coin" screen cancel after 2 minutes?

English: The coin slot has a 120-second (2-minute) safety countdown. If no coins are dropped within that window, the turn cancels automatically so other waiting students can use the coin slot. Simply tap "Insert Coins" again when you have your coins ready!

Tagalog: May 2-minute timer ang coinslot. Kapag walang naihulog sa loob ng 2 minuto, mag-ka-cancel ito para makagamit ang iba. Pindutin lang ulit ang "Insert Coins" kapag handa na ang barya.

Can I transfer my time to another phone or laptop?

English: Solo plans are locked to the specific phone or device that purchased them for security. If you want to share internet across 2 or more devices (or with friends/classmates), tap "Buy Group Plan" before inserting coins! You will get a 5-digit code for your other devices to connect.

Tagalog: Ang regular plan ay para sa isang device lamang. Kung gusto mong ibahagi sa kaibigan o sa laptop mo, piliin ang "Buy Group Plan" bago maghulog para makakuha ng 5-digit code.

How long is a Group Code valid?

English: Once generated, a Group Pass code can be redeemed anytime within 24 hours. Once a member joins, their timer starts and runs independently.

Tagalog: May 24 oras ang iyong mga kasama para ilagay ang 5-digit code sa kanilang portal page bago ito mag-expire.

What if my coin gets stuck or is not credited?

English: Don't panic! Use the Report Issue / Message Operator form below. Choose "Coin Stuck / Not Credited" and submit. The operator receives an instant notification with your phone's MAC address and timestamp to resolve it or add your time!

Tagalog: Huwag mag-alala! Gamitin ang form sa ibaba para magpadala ng mensahe sa admin/operator. Awtomatikong maipapadala ang iyong device info para ma-verify at ma-credit ang oras mo.

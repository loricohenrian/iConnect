How to Use iConnect

Follow these steps to get connected

1

Select a Plan

On the Home page, browse the available WiFi plans. Each plan displays its price and internet duration. Tap the plan that suits your needs.

2

Tap "Request Coin Slot"

After selecting a plan, tap the Request Coin Slot button. This activates the coin acceptor and reserves your turn to pay.

3

Insert Coins

Insert the required amount of coins into the machine. Wait for the system to confirm your payment — a message will appear once payment is complete.

4

Tap "Connect Now"

Once your payment is confirmed, tap the Connect Now button. Your device will be connected to the internet immediately. Enjoy browsing!

How to Use Group Plan

1

Create the Group

Tap Buy Group Plan, choose your plan, and input how many people will connect. Tap Request Coin Slot.

2

Insert Coins & Connect

Insert the total required coins. Once complete, tap Connect Now. Your timer will start and you will receive a 6-digit Group Code.

3

Share the Code

Tell the 6-digit Group Code to your friends or family members who are joining.

4

Join the Group

Your group members must open the portal, tap Have a group code? Enter it here, type the code, and tap Join Group to get internet access.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Tips

Quick answers to common questions about your WiFi session

Why did my timer disappear after reconnecting?

English: On newer iPhones (iOS 14+) and Android phones (Android 10+), your device turns on "Private Wi-Fi Address" or "Randomized MAC" by default. If your phone randomly changes its address, the system thinks you are a new device!

Fix: Go to your phone's Settings → Wi-Fi → Tap the (i) or gear icon next to iConnect → Set "MAC Address Type" to "Device MAC / Phone MAC". Reconnect and your remaining time will immediately reappear!

Tagalog: Sa mga bagong iPhone at Android, naka-on ang "Randomized MAC". Palitan ito sa Wi-Fi settings ng iyong phone patungong "Device MAC" para hindi mawala ang iyong natitirang oras.

Does my time keep running if I turn off Wi-Fi or leave?

English: Yes, your session timer counts down continuously in real-time unless you pause it. If you need to attend class, sleep, or step away, always tap "Pause Session" on your portal screen before disconnecting to freeze your minutes!

Tagalog: Opo, tuloy-tuloy ang bawas ng oras maliban kung i-pause mo ito. Pindutin ang "Pause" sa session screen bago umalis para hindi masayang ang iyong internet.

How does Pause work, and do I get more pauses when I extend?

English: Pausing freezes your remaining minutes so you can use them later. Paused time is safely kept for up to 24 to 72 hours (depending on your plan). YES! Whenever you extend your session with a new coin or plan, you automatically earn new pause chances!

Tagalog: Pwedeng i-pause ang oras hanggang 24-72 oras. Oo! Kapag naghulog ka ulit ng barya para mag-extend, madadagdagan muli ang iyong pause chances!

Why did the "Insert Coin" screen cancel after 2 minutes?

English: The coin slot has a 120-second (2-minute) safety countdown. If no coins are dropped within that window, the turn cancels automatically so other waiting students can use the coin slot. Simply tap "Request Coin Slot" again when you have your coins ready!

Tagalog: May 2-minute timer ang coinslot. Kapag walang naihulog sa loob ng 2 minuto, mag-ka-cancel ito para makagamit ang iba. Pindutin lang ulit ang "Request Coin Slot" kapag handa na ang barya.

Can I transfer my time to another phone or laptop?

English: Solo plans are locked to the specific phone or device that purchased them for security. If you want to share internet across 2 or more devices (or with friends/classmates), tap "Buy Group Plan" before inserting coins! You will get a 6-digit code for your other devices to connect.

Tagalog: Ang regular plan ay para sa isang device lamang. Kung gusto mong ibahagi sa kaibigan o sa laptop mo, piliin ang "Buy Group Plan" bago maghulog para makakuha ng 6-digit code.

How long is a Group Code valid?

English: Once generated, a Group Pass code can be redeemed anytime within 24 hours. Once a member joins, their timer starts and runs independently.

Tagalog: May 24 oras ang iyong mga kasama para ilagay ang 6-digit code sa kanilang portal page bago ito mag-expire.

What if my coin gets stuck or is not credited?

English: Don't panic! Use the Report Issue / Message Operator form below. Choose "Coin Stuck / Not Credited" and submit. The operator receives an instant notification with your phone's MAC address and timestamp to resolve it or add your time!

Tagalog: Huwag mag-alala! Gamitin ang form sa ibaba para magpadala ng mensahe sa admin/operator. Awtomatikong maipapadala ang iyong device info para ma-verify at ma-credit ang oras mo.
